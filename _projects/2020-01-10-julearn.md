In the same way as [seaborn](https://seaborn.pydata.org) provides an abstraction of [matplotlib’s](https://matplotlib.org) functionality aiming for powerful data visualization with minor coding, we built julearn on top of [scikit-learn](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/).

This library provides users with the possibility of testing ML models directly from [pandas](https://pandas.pydata.org) dataframes, while keeping the flexibiliy of using scikit-learn’s models.

Julearn is under active development but its already useful and we wanted to share it with the community. Feel free to try it and we will be happy to receive feedback.

Find more about Julearn in the [documentation](https://juaml.github.io/julearn/main/index.html).
